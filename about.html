About the shop

Good repairs begin
with good communication.

A local San Antonio paint and body shop for drivers looking to discuss vehicle damage and repair options.

Our approach

Respect your vehicle. Respect your time.

Choosing a body shop often comes after an unexpected and frustrating event. We want this website to make it easier to understand how to reach Navarro’s Paint and Body, where to find the shop, and how to start a repair conversation.

Customers who shared reviews highlighted clear explanations, reasonable pricing, and being kept informed. Those are useful standards to bring into any repair discussion: ask questions, understand the proposed work, and confirm timing and cost directly with the shop.

Business story, team biographies, years of experience, and any certifications should be added only after confirmation by the owner.
